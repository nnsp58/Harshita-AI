Text to Speech Converter

Convert your text into natural-sounding speech instantly. Our free online TTS tool uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis to generate audio from text. Perfect for accessibility, learning, and content creation.

How to Use

  1. Enter or paste the text you want to convert to speech
  2. Select a voice from the available options
  3. Adjust pitch if needed (optional)
  4. Click "Speak" button to hear the text

Features

  • Multiple Voices: Choose from various voices available on your system
  • Pitch Control: Adjust pitch from low (0) to high (2)
  • Offline Support: Works without internet after initial load
  • Mobile Friendly: Works on smartphones and tablets
  • No Registration: Completely free to use

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Text to Speech?

A: Text to Speech (TTS) is a technology that converts written text into spoken words. Our tool uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis capabilities to read text aloud.

Q: What voices are available?

A: The available voices depend on your operating system and browser. Common options include various accents and languages like English (US, UK), Hindi, Spanish, French, and more.

Q: Can I adjust the pitch?

A: Yes! Use the pitch slider to adjust from 0 (low) to 2 (high). Default is 1.0.

Q: Does Text to Speech work offline?

A: Yes! Our tool works completely offline after the page loads. It uses the browser's built-in speech synthesis engine.

Q: Can I use this on mobile devices?

A: Absolutely! The tool is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. Voice availability may vary by device.

🎙️ Microphone Permission Chahiye

Speech to Text kaam karne ke liye microphone access zaroori hai.
Neeche button dabao — browser ek popup dikhaega, usme "Allow" click karo.

Manually Allow Karne Ka Tarika:
  • 🌐 Chrome: Address bar mein 🔒 icon → Site Settings → Microphone → Allow
  • 🦊 Firefox: Address bar mein lock icon → Permissions → Microphone → Allow
  • 🧭 Edge: Address bar mein 🔒 → Permissions → Microphone → Allow
  • 📱 Android Chrome: ⋮ Menu → Settings → Site Settings → Microphone → Allow
  • 🍎 iOS Safari: Settings app → Safari → Microphone → Allow

Allow karne ke baad page refresh karein aur dobara try karein.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Text to Speech kya hai?

A: Text to Speech (TTS) ek technology hai jo written text ko bolne wali awaaz mein convert karti hai. Yeh tool browser ki built-in speech synthesis ka use karta hai.

Q: Speech to Text kya hai?

A: Speech to Text (STT) ek technology hai jo aapki bolne wali baat ko automatically written text mein convert kar deti hai — bilkul dictation jaisi. Yeh Web Speech API (Chrome/Edge) use karta hai.

Q: Kaunse voices available hain?

A: Available voices aapke operating system aur browser par depend karte hain. English (US, UK, Australian), Hindi, Spanish, French aur bahut si languages available hoti hain. Dropdown mein aapke system ke saare voices dikhenge.

Q: Kya Speech to Text offline kaam karta hai?

A: Speech to Text ke liye internet connection chahiye hota hai kyunki Chrome Google ke servers se recognition process karta hai. Text to Speech browser mein hi kaam karta hai aur offline bhi chalata hai.

Q: Kaunsa browser use karna chahiye?

A: Speech to Text ke liye Google Chrome ya Microsoft Edge best hai. Text to Speech sabhi modern browsers mein kaam karta hai.

Q: Kya main mobile pe use kar sakta hoon?

A: Haan! Dono tools mobile pe bhi kaam karte hain — Android Chrome aur iOS Safari mein. Voice availability device ke OS par depend karti hai.
